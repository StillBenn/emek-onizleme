Prostate Treatment with the Rezum Method: A Non-Surgical and Minimally Invasive Approach

One of the health problems men encounter as they get older is benign enlargement of the prostate gland. Known as Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH), this condition may show itself particularly through changes in urinary habits. Frequent urination, waking from sleep at night to go to the toilet, a weakening urine flow and the feeling of not being able to empty the bladder fully are among the symptoms of prostate enlargement that affect daily life.

Advances in medical technology have led to the development of alternatives that require less intervention for treating benign prostatic enlargement. One of these methods, Rezum water vapour therapy, is a minimally invasive procedure that aims to shrink prostate tissue in a controlled way.

Rezum technology, which may be considered as an alternative to traditional surgical methods, can help improve urine flow and reduce symptoms caused by prostate enlargement in suitable patients.

What Is Rezum Therapy?

Rezum is a minimally invasive method used to treat benign prostatic enlargement, which makes use of the controlled thermal energy of water vapour.

During the procedure, a specially designed device is advanced through the urinary channel to reach the prostate tissue. A controlled amount of water vapour is delivered to the selected areas. The heat energy carried by the water vapour creates a controlled tissue effect in the targeted prostate cells.

Over time, the body removes this affected tissue as part of its natural healing process. In this way, the aim is to reduce the pressure the prostate places on the urinary channel and to ease the passage of urine.

One of the important features of the Rezum method is that it is not necessary to surgically remove all of the prostate tissue during treatment.

How Is Rezum Prostate Treatment Performed?

The Rezum procedure is carried out under an appropriate method of anaesthesia, according to the patient's clinical condition and treatment plan.

The procedure generally consists of the following stages:

  1. First, the patient's prostate size, urinary symptoms and general urological condition are assessed.
  2. In patients considered suitable, the special Rezum device is advanced through the urinary channel to the prostate area.
  3. Controlled water vapour is applied to the enlarged areas of the prostate tissue.
  4. The heat effect produced by the water vapour causes cellular change in the targeted tissues.
  5. Over the following weeks and months, the volume of the prostate tissue is expected to decrease through the body's natural healing process.

After the procedure, some patients may need to use a urinary catheter temporarily. When the catheter is removed depends on the patient's condition and the doctor's assessment.

What Are the Advantages of Rezum Therapy?

Rezum technology may offer various advantages in the treatment of prostate enlargement in suitable patients.

A Minimally Invasive Method

Rezum therapy is delivered through the urinary channel without the need for an open surgical incision. This makes it a treatment option that requires less intervention than traditional surgical procedures.

May Help Keep the Risk of Bleeding More Limited

Treating prostate tissue with controlled water vapour energy offers a different approach from conventional tissue-removal methods. It may offer an advantage in terms of bleeding in suitable patients. However, the possibility of bleeding is not completely eliminated.

May Support the Preservation of Sexual Function

One of the notable features of the Rezum method is that it may offer an advantage in terms of preserving ejaculatory function in suitable patients.

Backward ejaculation (retrograde ejaculation), which can occur with some traditional prostate operations, is reported at lower rates after Rezum. However, the preservation of sexual function is not guaranteed and individual results may vary.

Aims to Improve Urine Flow

Reducing the pressure on the urinary channel narrowed by prostate enlargement is one of the main aims of Rezum therapy. After treatment, as the prostate tissue shrinks, urine flow may become stronger and urinary symptoms may lessen.

May Offer the Option of Short-Term Hospital Monitoring

In many patients, the Rezum procedure can be carried out as a day procedure or with short-term hospital monitoring. The patient's general health and post-procedure needs determine the time of discharge.

Has a Controlled Effect on Prostate Tissue

Water vapour is applied to the targeted areas of the prostate in a controlled way. This approach allows the treatment to focus on specific tissues.

Who Is Rezum Therapy Suitable For?

Rezum therapy may be considered particularly for some patients who have urinary symptoms caused by benign prostatic enlargement and who have not benefited sufficiently from medication.

However, not everyone with prostate enlargement is suitable for this method.

The following criteria are assessed before treatment:

  • The volume and anatomical structure of the prostate
  • Changes in urine flow rate
  • The amount of urine left in the bladder
  • The severity of symptoms caused by prostate enlargement
  • Medicines being taken and previous treatments
  • The patient's general health and treatment expectations

It is also important that the necessary investigations are carried out in patients with suspected prostate cancer. Rezum is not a treatment for prostate cancer; it is a treatment method for benign prostatic enlargement.

What Is Recovery Like After Rezum?

Because the effect of the procedure emerges over time, the recovery period after Rezum therapy may differ from that of some other prostate procedures.

In the first few days, there may be a burning sensation when urinating, frequent urination, slight blood in the urine or temporary difficulty urinating. These symptoms are mostly part of the recovery process; however, severe or persistent symptoms require a check-up by a doctor.

The effects of treatment can generally begin to be felt within a few weeks. The shrinking of the prostate tissue and the easing of urinary symptoms, however, may take a few months.

During the recovery period, it is important that patients:

  • Take the medicines recommended by the doctor regularly,
  • Follow the instructions given on catheter care,
  • Take the doctor's advice on adequate fluid intake into account,
  • Avoid heavy physical activity for the period specified by the doctor,
  • Do not miss their follow-up examinations.

The Differences Between Rezum and Traditional Prostate Operations

The methods used to treat prostate enlargement vary according to the patient's needs.

In traditional prostate operations, the enlarged prostate tissue may be removed using various surgical techniques. In the Rezum method, by contrast, the prostate tissue is treated with the thermal effect of water vapour and the tissue is expected to shrink over time.

The main differences between the two approaches are as follows:

  • Rezum is a minimally invasive procedure performed through the urinary channel.
  • Conventional surgical methods may involve the direct removal of prostate tissue.
  • After Rezum, recovery and the emergence of the treatment's effect may take time.
  • With some traditional surgical methods, relief in urine flow may occur earlier.
  • Effects on sexual function and ejaculation may vary depending on the method used.

Which method is preferred should be decided according to the size of the prostate, its anatomical features, and the patient's age, accompanying conditions and expectations.

Why Is Early Assessment Important in Prostate Enlargement?

Benign prostatic enlargement may affect urinary function over time. In some untreated patients, urine remaining in the bladder, recurrent urinary tract infections, bladder stones and problems that may affect kidney function can develop.

For this reason, it is important that men who experience changes in their urinary habits, particularly after the age of 50, consult a urology specialist.

Symptoms such as frequently getting up at night to urinate, difficulty starting urination, intermittent urination and the feeling that the bladder has not emptied completely should not be ignored.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rezum therapy a non-surgical method?

Rezum is a minimally invasive procedure performed through the urinary channel that does not require conventional open surgery. Technically, it is a treatment method that involves a surgical intervention.

How long does the Rezum procedure take?

The length of the procedure varies according to the patient's prostate structure and the extent of the treatment to be given. It is generally a procedure that can be completed in a short time.

Is a catheter fitted after Rezum therapy?

Yes, some patients may need to use a urinary catheter temporarily after the procedure. How long the catheter stays in place is decided by individual assessment.

Does Rezum affect your sex life?

Rezum may offer an advantage over some traditional prostate operations in terms of preserving ejaculatory function. However, no patient can be guaranteed that their sexual function will not be affected.

When are the effects of Rezum therapy seen?

In some patients, signs of improvement may appear in the first weeks. Assessing the full effect of the treatment can take up to a few months.

Does Rezum therapy remove the prostate completely?

No. The aim of Rezum therapy is not to remove the prostate completely but to ease the pressure on the urinary channel by reducing the volume of the benignly enlarged tissue.

Conclusion: Personalised and Modern Solutions in Prostate Treatment

Benign prostatic enlargement is one of the health problems that can significantly affect men's quality of life and that become more common with age.

With its minimally invasive approach, targeted tissue treatment and potential advantages for preserving ejaculatory function, Rezum water vapour therapy is one of the modern options that may be considered in prostate treatment.

However, every patient's prostate structure and treatment needs are different. A comprehensive urological assessment is therefore necessary to determine the most suitable method.

At the Urology Department of Emek Hospital Gaziantep, you can get detailed information about Rezum prostate treatment from Op. Dr. İbrahim Düzgün and find out which treatment options are suitable for your symptoms related to prostate enlargement.
