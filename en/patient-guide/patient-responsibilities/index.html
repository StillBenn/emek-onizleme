Responsibilities of Patients and Their Relatives

What we expect from patients and their relatives so that treatment goes smoothly.

Responsibility to Provide Information

  • Our patients should give accurate and complete information about their current complaints, past illnesses, whether they have been admitted to hospital, treatments received, medicines they take and other matters related to their health.
  • Our patients should inform their doctor of any changes, including unexpected changes, in their condition during and after treatment.
  • Our patients should let us know whether they have correctly understood what is expected of them regarding the procedures to be performed.

Responsibility to Follow Recommendations

  • Our patients are obliged to follow the treatment plan recommended by the doctor responsible for their treatment, and to accept the nurses and other healthcare staff carrying out the care plan in line with their doctor's instructions.

Responsibility for Refusing Planned Treatment

  • If the patient refuses the planned treatment or does not follow the doctor's instructions, the patient is responsible for the resulting consequences.

Responsibility to Pay for Tests and Treatment

  • When applying to our hospital, our patients should state how their healthcare costs will be covered and by which institution. Our patients are obliged to pay the amount due for diagnostic and treatment procedures within the period set by the institution.

Responsibility to Follow Hospital Rules and Practices

  • Our patients are obliged to follow the rules and practices set by our institution. These rules and practices are explained to the patient by healthcare staff following admission. (visiting, companions, etc.)

Responsibility to Show Respect

  • Our patients are obliged to respect the rights of other patients and healthcare staff. In situations at our hospital that endanger patients, such as noise, smoke or too many visitors, they must comply with the measures taken by hospital management.

Responsibility to Report Infectious Diseases

  • Patients diagnosed with or suspected of having an infectious disease should not request discharge unless the authorised doctor has given “permission stating that there is no objection to their leaving”.

Responsibility Not to Make Inappropriate Requests

  • Our patients should not request any medicine or procedure that the authorised doctor has not approved and that is not part of the treatment plan.

Responsibility to Follow Visiting Rules

  • Our patients are responsible for receiving their visitors within the rules set by our hospital. It is among the patient's responsibilities to ensure that visitors do not bring food or drink in any form, that the number of visitors is limited, that visits are kept short, and that visitors do not use belongings of the patient or of other people.

Responsibility to Compensate

  • Our patients are obliged to compensate for any loss or damage they cause to our hospital's fixtures, equipment and consumables through misuse or intentionally.
