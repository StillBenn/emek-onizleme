Online appointment

Choose the department and doctor, and tell us the day that suits you. Your request is prepared; our call centre will call you to confirm your appointment.

  • About one minute
  • Our call centre will call you
  • Your details are used only for your appointment

Step 1 / 5

Which department would you like to book an appointment with?

Type the name of the department or your symptom — for example “knee pain” — and we will show you the right department.

You don't need an appointment for an emergency: our Emergency Department is open 24/7 — 0342 320 02 02. If life is in danger, call 112.

Step 2 / 5

Choose your doctor

If you have no preference for a doctor, choose “No preference”; you will be directed to our first available doctor.

Doctor selection for this department is not available online; continue with “No preference” and our call centre will direct you to a suitable doctor.

Step 3 / 5

Which day suits you?

Choose a day within the next three weeks. The exact time is set once our call centre confirms it.
