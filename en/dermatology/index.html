Department

Dermatology

In the Dermatology (Skin Diseases) Department, skin, hair and nail diseases, sores and skin changes in the mouth and genital area, allergic diseases and cosmetic problems are evaluated through dermatological examination and technological methods.

Book an appointment0342 320 02 02

Department doctors

Spec. Dr. M. Çağlar SoysalDermatology

To make a definitive diagnosis of some skin diseases, it may be necessary to take a tissue sample from the skin by punch biopsy.

Radiosurgery and the cautery unit are used for skin growths called papillomas, while cryotherapy (freezing treatment) is used for warts and certain skin problems. In our department, a skin patch test (true test) and a prick test are performed for diagnostic purposes in allergic skin diseases. Allergy tests are also carried out on blood, and moles and skin cancers (malignant melanoma) are evaluated.

As a result of this examination, it may be decided to monitor some moles and follow-up appointments are given; moles found suitable for removal are removed surgically using appropriate methods.
