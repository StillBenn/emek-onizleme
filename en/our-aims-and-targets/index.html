Our Aims and Targets

To provide our patients with the highest-quality, most reliable healthcare in line with ethical values.

At Emek Hospital Gaziantep, we aim to provide our patients with the best healthcare. In line with this goal, we have the following aim and targets:

Our Aim

As Emek Hospital Gaziantep, our aim is to provide our patients with the highest-quality, reliable healthcare in line with ethical values, and, while doing so, to continually develop and renew ourselves using the latest technologies.

  • To be the hospital of choice in our region by providing reliable and easily accessible healthcare.
  • To increase our patients' satisfaction.
  • To increase our employees' satisfaction.
  • To provide appropriate services in accordance with the law and ethical principles while fulfilling our responsibilities.
  • To remedy shortcomings in the hospital's infrastructure and equipment and to ensure continuity.
  • To raise the quality of service by improving our employees' qualifications.
  • To ensure the improvement of priority processes for quality and patient safety.
  • To be an environmentally conscious hospital that cares about environmental health and reduces its waste.
  • To be a hospital that strives for continuous improvement.

Our Goals

  • To make it easier for people with special needs, whether due to physical, mental, social or other circumstances, to access appropriate healthcare.
  • To hold certification for health tourism.
  • To establish partnerships with private health insurance providers.
  • To identify needs based on our patients' feedback and suggestions.
  • To identify needs based on our employees' feedback and suggestions.
  • To act in accordance with institutional legislation and regulations.
  • To provide high-quality, uninterrupted healthcare by minimising shortcomings in the hospital's infrastructure.
  • In-service training, certification, and assigning staff to suitable departments according to their abilities.
  • Increasing employee training and awareness.
  • Regular training on separating waste at source, and creating suitable and sufficient collection areas for waste.
  • To provide services at the most reasonable cost by combining technological changes and changes in the healthcare sector with quality.
  • To provide sound services with regard to patient/relative satisfaction, rights and safety.
  • To deliver our services on time, systematically, in a planned manner and with as few errors as possible.
  • To ensure continuous improvement without compromising on Quality Management System standards.
  • To be a hospital that values and safeguards patient privacy.
  • To provide services based on continuous development and measurement, in terms of both quality and quantity.
  • To improve service quality through continuous measurement and evaluation.
  • To increase the quality/efficiency of healthcare and ensure its sustainability.

At Emek Hospital Gaziantep, as a healthcare institution, we are constantly developing ourselves to meet the needs of society. In line with our goals, we aim to provide high-quality, reliable services that respect ethical values, focusing on our patients' health and safety. We will continue to provide high-quality, innovative services in the healthcare sector.
