Neurology

Book an appointment

In addition to diagnostic and short-term treatment services, our hospital also provides long-term treatment of chronic neurological diseases with a multidisciplinary approach. We provide diagnosis and treatment for the most common complaints in the community, such as headache, migraine and dizziness, as well as for neurological diseases such as stroke, epilepsy, Multiple Sclerosis, Parkinson's Disease and Alzheimer's Disease.

At our hospital, the EEG and EMG examinations used in diagnosing neurological diseases are also performed by experienced specialists.
